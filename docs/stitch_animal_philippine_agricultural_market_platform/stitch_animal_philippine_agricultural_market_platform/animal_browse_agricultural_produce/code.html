🌱 AniMAL Direct Order Request & Fulfillment Protocol

AniMAL connects buyers directly with local farmers. Orders are submitted as direct requests. When delivery is selected, delivery fees are quoted by the farmer for your mutual confirmation prior to order preparation. Payments and product handovers are coordinated directly between the registered buyer and the farmer producer upon harvest verification.
