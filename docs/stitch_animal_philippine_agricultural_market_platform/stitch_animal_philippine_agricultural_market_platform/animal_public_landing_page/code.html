Ordering Guide

For Local Buyers

1

Browse Produce

Explore available local vegetables, fruits, and crops organized by province, municipality, and barangay.

2

Submit Request

Select your needed quantities in kilos or sacks and submit a simple order request along with your fulfillment preference.

3

Review Delivery Terms

If you requested delivery, review the farmer's quoted local transport fee directly, or opt for free scheduled farm gate pickup.

4

Receive Fresh Harvest

Inspect the fresh harvest at your doorstep or farm pickup point and settle according to the farmer's local arrangement.

Farmer Onboarding

For Filipino Farmers

1

Register Farm Profile

Create your agricultural producer profile and submit basic barangay location and farm parcel details.

2

Get Admin Verified

Platform administrators validate your farm details and credentials to maintain mutual community safety and credibility.

3

Post Harvest Batches

List your current or incoming harvest with available kilograms, farmgate price per kg/bundle, and pick-up site.

4

Accept & Fulfill Requests

Review direct requests from buyers, state any local delivery fee if requested, prepare produce, and fulfill smoothly.
