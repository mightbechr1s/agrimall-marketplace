## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted specifically for agricultural commerce in the Philippines, bridging Filipino smallholders, agricultural cooperatives, and direct community buyers. The visual narrative combines grounded agrarian heritage with intuitive digital marketplace utilities. 

The emotional tone balances deep institutional trust, direct-from-earth freshness, and straightforward clarity. It deliberately shuns cold Silicon Valley minimalism, sterile enterprise SaaS tropes, and loud, hyper-stimulating retail discount interfaces. Instead, the interface establishes a tactile, warm, and highly functional aesthetic:
- **Agrarian Authenticity:** Warm organic stone and linen surfaces replace synthetic cold grays, evoking the tactile nature of dried grain, woven bamboo baskets (*bayong*), and sun-warmed clay soil.
- **Dignified Utility:** High-contrast typographic hierarchy and oversized touch targets accommodate field conditions, outdoor sunlight, varying digital literacy levels, and low-spec mobile devices.
- **Direct Trust:** Clear verification artifacts, unmistakable state badges, and prominent currency notation (₱) establish confidence in transactions between rural producers and urban buyers.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the Philippine landscape—volcanic soil, lush tropical terraces, and sun-ripened produce.

### Primary: Deep Agricultural Forest Green (`#1B4D3E`)
- Deep shade: `#143D32`
- Main surface/brand token: `#1B4D3E`
- Represents fertility, grounded stability, and cooperative authority. Used for primary navigation, main call-to-action buttons, high-priority icons, and primary headers.

### Secondary: Fresh Harvest Green (`#2E7D32`)
- Vibrant tier: `#388E3C`
- Light tint: `#4CAF50`
- Surface tint: `#DCFCE7` (text: `#16A34A`)
- Used for positive feedback loops, verified farmer accreditation, inventory availability, and fresh produce status.

### Tertiary & Accent: Warm Terracotta & Sun Harvest (`#C06C3E`)
- Terracotta deep: `#C06C3E`
- Clay vibrant: `#D97736`
- Golden Wheat: `#D4A373` / `#F4A261`
- Accent token for harvest alerts, wholesale bulk callouts, featured seasonal crop badges, and primary action contrasts.

### Neutrals & Backgrounds
- Base Canvas (Soft Organic Warm Off-White): `#FAF8F5`
- Surface Container (Warm Cream): `#F5F2EB`
- Elevated Container (Linen Card): `#FFFFFF`

- Structural Border: `#E2DED6`

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is specified across all levels for its humanist geometric clarity, wide aperture, and generous x-height. These characteristics ensure legible rendering on budget mobile screens in direct sunlight.

### Implementation Guidelines
- **Currency Typography:** All price displays require `price-display` paired with bold formatting for the Philippine Peso symbol (`₱`). The peso sign must always match the baseline and weight of the numerical value without floating superscript styling.
- **Form Readability:** Labels never drop below `label-lg` (0.875rem) with semi-bold weight (`fontWeight: 600`) to ensure legibility during field entry.
- **Bilingual Nuance:** Descriptions and cooperative tags frequently combine Tagalog/Cebuano/Ilocano and English (e.g., *Palay*, *Saging na Saba*, *Sitaw*). Keep line-height generous (`1.5` to `1.6`) to accommodate diverse sentence structures and prevent visual crowding.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model employs an 8pt base grid within a responsive fluid column system:
- **Mobile (320px – 639px):** 4-column layout, 16px (`gutter-mobile`) outer margins. Touch targets strictly observe `touch-target-min` (48px / `3rem`) to support field ergonomics.
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px):** 8-column layout, 24px (`gutter-tablet`) margins. Grid cards shift from full-width stacking to 2-column masonry or tabular rows.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with a maximum container boundary of 1280px, centered with 32px (`gutter-desktop`) gutters.

### Form Factor Reflow
- Marketplace listings compress to a single high-information-density feed with persistent sticky filters on mobile, moving to a side-panel filter drawer on tablet and a left rail on desktop.
- Cart and negotiation workflows maintain a sticky bottom action bar on mobile (`space-md` padding), ensuring the primary order action is accessible with one hand.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical-tactile layering, soft organic contrast, and warm earth-toned shadows instead of sharp greyscale drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to base canvas (`#FAF8F5`). No shadow, no outline.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Applied to product cards, farmer profile panels, and filter modules. `#FFFFFF` background with a subtle border of `1px solid #E2DED6` and a warm low-opacity ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Sticky Menus):** Applied to hovered produce cards, floating transaction trays, and sticky headers: `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.03)`. Notice the tinted ambient green-black shadow.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Bottom Drawers):** Applied to order confirmation sheets, crop dispatch modals, and filter bottom-sheets on mobile: `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(20, 61, 50, 0.14), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(20, 61, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Depressed / Inset States):** Form inputs, weight quantity counters, and tapped card states use an inset flat treatment: `background-color: #EFECE6` with an inner border of `#D5CFBE`. No inner shadow.

## Shapes

The roundedness language reflects natural agricultural forms—seeds, leaves, and organic produce shapes. 

- Base UI components (standard buttons, text inputs, dropdown triggers) use `rounded-lg` (0.75rem to 1rem).
- Produce cards, wholesale dispatch panels, and modal containers use `rounded-xl` (1rem to 1.5rem).
- Interactive pills, badges, status chips, and quantity increment steppers use fully circular pill contours (`rounded-full` / 9999px).
- Strictly avoid rigid, razor-sharp rectangular aesthetics (0px) as they contradict the brand's organic positioning.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Field Green):** Solid `#1B4D3E` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-lg`, min-height 48px. Active tap state scales down to `0.98` with background tint shifting to `#143D32`.
- **Secondary Action (Warm Terracotta / Harvest):** `#C06C3E` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for time-critical transactional verbs like "Place Bulk Order", "Accept Bid", or "Confirm Harvest Date".
- **Outline / Quiet Action:** `1.5px solid #E2DED6`, text `#1F2923`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active state uses `#F5F2EB`.

### "✓ Verified Farmer" Badge
- **Container:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 10px 4px 8px`.
- **Colors:** Background `#DCFCE7`, border `1px solid #BBF7D0`, text `#15803D`.
- **Iconography:** Leading checkmark or stylized sprout icon enclosed in a solid `#16A34A` mini-circle with white check. Font size `label-md` (0.75rem, bold).

### Produce Listing Cards
- **Structure:** Vertical card on mobile, flexible horizontal or modular grid on desktop.
- **Visuals:** Top or left container hosts photographic asset (1:1 aspect ratio) with a subtle inset warm border.
- **Content Flow:** 
  1. Origin pill badge (`#EFECE6` bg, `#58695E` text, e.g., "Benguet" or "Bukidnon").
  2. Produce Title (`headline-sm`, `#1F2923`).
  3. Producer line paired with the Verified Farmer badge.
  4. Pricing row: `price-display` in `#1B4D3E` (e.g., `₱65 / kg`) anchored adjacent to wholesale availability metadata (`Min. 50 kg`).

### Form Inputs & Quantity Steppers
- **Text Inputs:** Solid warm linen background (`#EFECE6`), transitioning to `#FFFFFF` on focus with a crisp `2px` ring in `#1B4D3E`. Labels rendered in `label-lg` with high contrast (`#1F2923`).
- **Quantity Stepper (Sack / Kg / Crate increments):** Unified pill structure. Left `-` and right `+` circular buttons (minimum 44px hit-box) flanking an editable bold numerical center.

### Status Chips
- Status representations strictly follow the operational state matrix:
  - **Pending:** `#FEF3C7` background, `#D97706` text and dot indicator.
  - **Verified / Confirmed:** `#DCFCE7` background, `#16A34A` text and check indicator.
  - **In Transit / Dispatched:** `#CCFBF1` background, `#0D9488` text and cargo icon indicator.
  - **Cancelled / Disrupted:** `#FEE2E2` background, `#DC2626` text and cross indicator.

### Philippine Peso Price Tag Token
- Currency symbol (`₱`) must always match numeric font-family (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), weight (`800`), and color. Never replace with "PHP" inside compact product listings; retain "PHP" only for downloadable legal receipts and bank remittance summaries.
