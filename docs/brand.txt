AgriMALL brand guide (v5 — Agrimall palette)
==================================================

Positioning
  Direct from verified Filipino farms. Farm to table, no middlemen.
  Revolutionizing agriculture: premium products & expert support.

Logo
  Mark: a green tractor (primary base #1b4332, secondary accent #2d6a4f).
  Wordmark: "AgriMALL" in Fraunces 700, letter-spacing -0.015em, primary ink.

  Files (public/assets/branding/)
    mark.svg             tractor only, forest + emerald. Header, sidebar, favicon, app icon.
    mark-reverse.svg     tractor only, light gray on charcoal. Dark backgrounds.
    logo-horizontal.svg  tractor + wordmark, forest. Auth pages, documents.
    logo-reverse.svg     tractor + wordmark, light gray. Footer, dark panels.
    logo-stacked.svg     tractor above wordmark + tagline. Square placements.
    logo-one-color.svg   outline only, single ink. Print, stamps, watermarks.
    logo-formal.svg      horizontal + tagline. Letterheads, invoices.

  Clear space: at least half the mark's width on every side.
  Minimum size: mark 16px, horizontal lockup 120px wide.
  Never: recolor the tractor outside the palette, stretch, outline the wordmark,
  place the forest logo on a dark background, or add shadows.

Colour

    paper         #f8f9fa   cards, fields
    primary       #1b4332   deep forest — text, links, icons, filled surfaces
    primary-dark  #0d2818   footer, hover
    accent        #2d6a4f   emerald — secondary actions, badges, success
    gold          #2d6a4f   (alias for accent)
    gold-text     #1b4332   accent as text on light surfaces
    text          #212529   charcoal
    text-soft     #6c757d   slate gray (subtitles, timestamps)
    borders       #dee2e6   light gray separators
    field-border  #6c757d   inputs (>=3:1 on white)

    success       #52b788
    focus-ring    #52b788
    trust band    #2d6a4f   filled band with light labels

  Rules
    Primary is never body text on dark surfaces — use --text.
    Filled brand surfaces use primary (light) or #2d6a4f (dark); text on them is white.
    Borders on interactive fields must clear 3:1; card separators may stay soft.
    No neon, no gradients as primary colour.

Typography
  Display / headlines  Fraunces (bundled, offline). 600-700, tight tracking.
  Body / UI            Source Sans Pro. 400-600.
  Mono / figures       JetBrains Mono.
  Headlines are sentence case. Labels and section kickers are uppercase,
  0.08em tracking, 0.7-0.75rem.

Spacing and shape
  8px base grid. Section padding 5rem desktop / 3.5rem mobile.
  Cards: 18px radius, 1px border, soft warm shadow.
  Buttons: pill radius. Pills: pill radius, 1px border.
