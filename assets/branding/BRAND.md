# AniMAL Logo Kit

The field-gate mark keeps a bold agricultural `A` as its primary silhouette. Two restrained cream furrows add cultivated-field detail, while the rounded clay bridge and cream center mark the point where farmers connect with buyers and market opportunities.

## Files

- `logo-horizontal.svg`: default logo for application headers and general use
- `logo-stacked.svg`: centered or square compositions
- `mark.svg`: favicon, avatar, and compact contexts where AniMAL is already named
- `logo-formal.svg`: capstone covers and formal exhibits using the complete project title
- `logo-one-color.svg`: single-ink printing, engraving, and restricted-color use
- `logo-reverse.svg`: light one-color treatment for rice-green backgrounds

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the diameter of the cream exchange point.
- Minimum widths: 24 px for the mark, 120 px for the horizontal logo, and 180 px for the formal lockup.
- Prefer harvest cream (`#f6f0e4`) or white backgrounds.
- Use rice green (`#1f5a3d`) as the primary color and clay (`#ad5734`) only for the market bridge and `MAL` emphasis.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, or rearrange the mark.

The SVG wordmarks use Atkinson Hyperlegible with Trebuchet MS as a fallback. Install or bundle Atkinson Hyperlegible before final production export to preserve consistent wordmark metrics.
